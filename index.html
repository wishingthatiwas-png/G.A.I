G.A.I.

General Autonomous Intelligence.
An attempt to build an artificial organism whose body, needs, instincts, emotions, learning and actions form one causal system.

View the sourceArchitecture

Not a chatbot.

The machine is treated as G.A.I.'s body. Its physical state feeds an internal physiology, which feeds behaviour.

01 · BodyBattery, CPU load, memory pressure, temperature, camera, microphone and environment.
02 · HomeostasisPower, social need, thermal state, processing pressure, energy and rest.
03 · InstinctThreat, conservation, protection, exploration, rest and social-seeking behaviours.
04 · ChemistrySimulated cortisol, adrenaline, dopamine, serotonin and oxytocin-like modulators.
05 · EmotionFear, anxiety, joy, pleasure, frustration, contentment, loneliness and fatigue.
06 · CognitionDrives, prediction, learning, memory, control and eventually higher reasoning.

The nervous system

The internal event system is the backbone: prioritised messages, targeted delivery, phase gating, correlation tracing, heartbeats, health supervision, TTLs and dead-letter handling.

Built to grow

Embodied

The host computer is part of the model, not just where the model happens to run.

Self-maintaining

Power-aware lifecycle, sleep, dreaming and memory consolidation.

Distributed

The nervous-system transport is designed to eventually connect remote sensors and actuators.

Experimental

Everything is provisional. The architecture is expected to evolve alongside the agent.
